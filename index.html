Project Mission: Formulating scientifically tested infant flour to combat childhood malnutrition and promote healthy cognitive development.

Scientific Approach to Child Growth

Malnutrition in early childhood leads to irreversible developmental delays. Imikurire provides a balanced, highly digestible macro and micronutrient profile derived entirely from locally available organic produce.

🧬

Bio-Fortification

Enhancing nutrient bioavailability through controlled roasting, germinating, and precise proportioning of plant proteins and essential minerals.

🌾

Local Supply Chain

Sourced directly from Rwandan farmers, empowering local agricultural cooperatives while maintaining rigorous quality control standards.

📈

Measurable Impact

Designed to meet WHO complementary feeding guidelines, targeting reduced stunting rates across rural and urban communities.
