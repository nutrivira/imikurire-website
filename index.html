Scientific Pillars

Driven by Science, Proven by Results

Combining traditional local nutrition with advanced biotechnology to optimize bioavailability and combat chronic malnutrition.

Triple-Activation Bio-Fortification

Utilizing controlled germination, microbial fermentation, and enzymatic treatment of local grains to reduce anti-nutrients (phytates) and unlock essential iron, zinc, and protein.

  • Reduced Phytate Inhibition
  • Enhanced Mineral Bioavailability

Clinical Biomarker Monitoring

Evaluating gut enteropathy and mucosal healing via non-invasive biomarker assays (such as Fecal Calprotectin), tracking real health status beyond simple weight indicators.

  • Gut Mucosal Health Audits
  • Quantitative Inflammatory Tracking

Community Scalable Impact

Empowering local smallholders and healthcare centers with low-cost processing models, creating sustainable nutrition ecosystems directly within targeted communities.

  • Local Value Chain Integration
  • High-Adherence Food Formats

Project Timeline

Implementation Roadmap

From laboratory development to multi-district deployment across Rwanda and beyond.

Phase 1 (Completed)

Formulation & Triple-Activation Lab Testing

Optimization of bio-fortification parameters using local sorghum, beans, and sweet potato varieties. Verified phytate reduction levels exceeding 70% in lab trials.

Phase 2 (Current)
Active Clinical Pilot

Biomarker Validation Study

Conducting cohort monitoring across selected healthcare centers in Rwanda. Evaluating Fecal Calprotectin levels and length-for-age progression over a 6-month trial window.

Phase 3 (Upcoming)

District-Level Community Scaling

Expanding processing facilities and distribution networks to reach over 15,000 households, integrating local farmer cooperatives into the supply chain.
