Scientific Pillars

Driven by Science, Proven by Results

Combining traditional local food systems with advanced biotechnology to optimize nutrient uptake.

Triple-Activation Processing

Controlled germination, microbial fermentation, and bio-fortification reduce anti-nutrients (phytates) and maximize micro-nutrient absorption.

  • Reduced Phytate Inhibition
  • Superior Bioavailability

Biomarker Clinical Monitoring

Tracking environmental enteric dysfunction (EED) and mucosal recovery using Fecal Calprotectin assays and growth indices.

  • Non-Invasive Diagnostics
  • Evidence-Based Growth Outcomes

Community Scalable Model

Empowering smallholder farmers, healthcare centers, and caregiver networks across Rwanda with accessible bio-fortified nutritional security.

  • Agricultural Integration
  • Scalable Distribution

Implementation Roadmap

From formulation research to community-wide scaling across Rwanda.

Phase 1 (Completed)

Formulation & Phytate Reduction Trials

Development of bio-fortification processing parameters using local sorghum, bean, and sweet potato varieties to unlock bioavailable iron and zinc.

Phase 2 (Active)
Community Pilot

Rusasa & ECD Pilot Implementation

Field testing complementary porridge and kitchen garden models while establishing quantitative biomarker monitoring protocols for gut health evaluation.

Phase 3 (Scaling)

District-Level Operations & Regulatory Certification

Expansion of food processing capacity, regulatory compliance certification, and distribution partnerships with health centers and cooperatives.
